# Privacy and publication audit

The v0.9 publication set was checked for the two private project identifiers and their common case and separator variants. The scan covered filenames, documentation, KiCad project and board files, project-local footprints, BOM, verification reports, the KiCad source diff, and each Gerber and drill file inside the distribution archives. Both sides of the PCB artwork were also reviewed visually.

**Result:** zero matches in the v0.9 publication set. The legacy v0.8 ZIP referenced by the repository was checked separately by extracting and scanning its contents.
