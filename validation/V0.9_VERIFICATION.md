## Jumper and read-pinout verification

The 25 common pins were checked against the DIP pinouts. Every documented matrix row was resolved through the actual PCB jumper pad nets to its read-mode signal. H26 supplies A13 in the 27C128 and 27C256 rows. H27 supplies A14 in the 27C256 rows.

| Host | Memory | P1, P26, P27 | Resolved at U1 pins 1, 26, 27 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27C64 | 27C256 / SST27SF256 | VCC, A13, A14 | VPP at VCC, A13, A14 |
| 27C64 | AT28C256 | A14, A13, VCC | A14, A13, /WE high |
| 27C64 | 27C512 / SST27SF512 | A15, A13, A14 | A15, A13, A14 |
| 27C128 | 27C256 / SST27SF256 | VCC, H26, A14 | VPP at VCC, A13, A14 |
| 27C128 | AT28C256 | A14, H26, VCC | A14, A13, /WE high |
| 27C128 | 27C512 / SST27SF512 | A15, H26, A14 | A15, A13, A14 |
| 27C256 | 27C256 / SST27SF256 | VCC, H26, H27 | VPP at VCC, A13, A14 |
| 27C256 | AT28C256 | H27, H26, VCC | A14, A13, /WE high |
| 27C256 | 27C512 / SST27SF512 | A15, H26, H27 | A15, A13, A14 |

U1 pin 22 carries active-low output enable for every row. On SST27SF512 and ST M27C512, that pin doubles as a programming-voltage input. The matrix applies to normal read-mode signals. The programming voltage belongs at the programmer. H1 pin 1 is isolated by design.

Reference pinouts and read modes: [Microchip AT27C256R](https://www.microchip.com/content/dam/mchp/documents/OTH/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/doc0014.pdf), [Microchip AT28C256](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MPD/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/AT28C256-Industrial-Grade-256-Kbit-Paged-Parallel-EEPROM-Data-Sheet-DS20006386.pdf), [ST M27C512](https://media.digikey.com/pdf/Data%20Sheets/ST%20Microelectronics%20PDFS/M27C512.pdf), [SST27SF256 and SST27SF512, Figure 3 and Tables 3–4](https://deramp.com/downloads/mfe_archive/050-Component%20Specifications/SST/SST27SF256-27SF512.pdf), [ST M27C64A](https://www.alldatasheet.com/html-pdf/22828/STMICROELECTRONICS/M27C64A/1621/1/M27C64A.html), [ST M27C128A](https://www.eliptor.pl/pub/pdf/ST/M27C128.pdf).
